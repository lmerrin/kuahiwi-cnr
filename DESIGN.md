# Kuahiwi Bicycle Care and Repair LLC - v1

## Intent
Standalone recreation for GitHub preview and possible Cloudflare Pages deployment; not a Wix source export or a pixel-exact copy. No deployment is included.

## Brand
Colors: oxide #A3382F; deep blue #1D323F; warm cream #F8EDDD; soft gray #D7D6D2. Existing white gear badge. Edo SZ for headings; Rockwell Bold for subheadings; Rockwell Nova Light for body. Body minimum 17px; generous leading. Supplied fonts must have appropriate website licensing before production.

## Structure
Single-page service website: hero, services, complete pricing, process, bike types, vintage work/projects, owner, testimonials, all 15 FAQ entries, contact and footer. Supporting privacy page and 404 page. Clear estimate CTA and phone links. Real imagery retrieved from the existing website; no generated owner or project images.

## Presentation
Cream field, navy typography, oxide actions, neutral dividers. Editorial service lists and photographic panels; restrained transitions. Responsive one-column layout on phones, accessible menu and native FAQ disclosures. No ornamental mountains, waves, emojis, or invented credentials.

## Behavior and constraints
The current Wix upload/form backend cannot be copied from public content. This package's inquiry form explicitly prepares an email in the visitor's mail app; it does not send/store inquiries and cannot accept uploads. No backend keys. No analytics or tracking added. No claims of final legal compliance. Privacy copy is a review draft explaining this implementation.

## Review before launch
Confirm business name, email (retrievals differed), hours, rates, Rice Street Retros relationship, testimonials, shipping, pickup/delivery, service promises and warranty. Verify font web-use rights. Replace email-draft workflow with an approved form endpoint if desired. Do not publish draft policies unreviewed.

## Technical
Semantic HTML, relative file links, local compressed photos/fonts, small CSS/JS, skip link and focus states. Metadata, favicon, sitemap, robots, 404 and Cloudflare headers included. Preview pages use noindex until explicitly made launch-ready. Local automated structural checks; no browser QA available in this environment.
