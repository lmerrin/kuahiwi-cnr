Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Kuahiwi Bicycle Care and Repair LLC respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and handle information when you visit www.kuahiwibikecnr.com or contact us about our services.

Information you provide

When you contact us, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, bicycle details, service requests, photographs, and other information you choose to share.

Please only send information relevant to your inquiry. Do not include payment card details, identification documents, or other sensitive personal information in website inquiries or email attachments.

Website inquiries and photographs

Our website’s inquiry form prepares a message in your email application. Entering information into the form does not submit it to Kuahiwi. We receive your information only when you send the email.

You may attach photographs or other relevant files through your email application. Your email provider and our email provider process these communications under their own privacy practices.

We use photographs you provide to understand your inquiry, assess your bicycle’s condition, and discuss possible services. We will ask for your permission before using your submitted photographs in public marketing materials.

Information collected automatically

Our website hosting and security providers may process technical information when you visit the website, such as your IP address, browser and device information, requested pages, and the date and time of your visit.

This information supports website delivery, troubleshooting, reliability, and security.

How we use your information

We use information to:

  • Respond to questions and service requests.
  • Prepare estimates and discuss repairs, restorations, or custom builds.
  • Arrange service, shipping, pickup, or delivery when applicable.
  • Communicate about your bicycle and work in progress.
  • Maintain relevant customer and service records.
  • Protect our website and business from spam, misuse, and unauthorized activity.
  • Meet applicable legal and recordkeeping obligations.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with service providers that support our business, such as website hosting, email, and shipping providers, when needed to carry out the purposes described in this policy.

We may also disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect our rights, address fraud or misuse, or protect someone’s safety.

Cookies and tracking

We do not use this website for targeted advertising or cross-site advertising tracking.

Our hosting or security providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver and protect the website. You can manage cookies through your browser settings, although blocking certain technologies may affect website functionality.

When you use the language toggle, your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage so the website can remember your preference. You can clear this preference through your browser settings.

Information retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to respond to inquiries, provide services, maintain relevant business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations.

Retention periods vary depending on the information and its purpose. Copies may remain in routine backups until those backups are replaced or deleted.

Information security

We take reasonable measures to protect information we handle. However, no website, email service, or electronic storage system can guarantee complete security.

Your choices

You can choose not to provide personal information, although we may need certain details to respond to your inquiry or provide a requested service.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have provided, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before responding. Some information may need to be retained for legal or legitimate business purposes.

Links to other websites

Our website may link to other businesses or services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. We are not responsible for how those websites collect or use information.

Children’s privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us so we can address it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our website, services, or information practices change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised date.
